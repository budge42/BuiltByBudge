Overview

Smarter Choices, Made Simple.

IngreedyIt lets users scan food products and instantly understand what’s inside them. Ingredients are decoded, ranked and explained in plain language. It highlights additives, preservatives and marketing tricks, helping people make informed decisions quickly. The app focuses on education, empowering better choices without fear or confusion.
